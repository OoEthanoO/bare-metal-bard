WHAT THIS SHOWS. On the 5070 Ti the same kernel read best 11100 / median 11085
-- they agreed to 0.1%, because that machine was quiet. Here they split apart:
`best` catches the iteration that happened to run while the desktop was idle,
`median` carries the contention tax. A benchmark that reports only `best` would
have hidden this entirely.

The absolute level is depressed too, and by about the right amount. cuBLAS
reads ~9100 here against the 5070 Ti's ~9490, despite this card having 60 SMs
against 46 at the same clock. Correcting for the ~22% the desktop takes,
9100/0.78 = 11700, which is 1.23x the 5070 Ti -- close to the 1.30x the SM
counts predict. The deficit is the desktop, not the card.
